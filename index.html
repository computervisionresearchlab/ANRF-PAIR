About the Programme

Advancing Research Through Partnership

Strengthening research capabilities through collaboration, knowledge exchange, and interdisciplinary innovation.

The Anusandhan National Research Foundation Partnerships for Accelerated Innovation and Research (ANRF PAIR) programme is designed to foster a collaborative and inclusive research ecosystem by strengthening research capabilities and promoting interdisciplinary collaboration among higher education institutions.

National Institute of Technology Rourkela serves as the hub institution for a network of seven partner institutions. Through this hub-and-spoke model, the consortium facilitates knowledge exchange, capacity building, shared research infrastructure, joint academic activities, and access to advanced expertise.

Under this initiative, NIT Rourkela leads the Consortium of Technologies for Sustainable Agriculture, Health, Energy and Environment (SAHEE Consortium), focusing on four interconnected thematic areas.
